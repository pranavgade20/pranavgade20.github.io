Privacy Policy

This privacy policy covers personal apps and tools published by Pranav Gade at pranavg.me, including any apps that use Google Sign-In or call Google APIs on a user's behalf ("the apps").

Last updated: 2026-05-30.

What the apps do with your data

The apps access your data only with your explicit authorization, and only the scopes you grant. Data accessed via Google APIs is used solely to provide the user-facing features you invoke. It is not transmitted to any third party, not used for advertising, not used for analytics or profiling, and not used to train any machine learning model.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage

The apps minimize what they store. Where credentials or user content are retained, they are kept locally on the device or environment where the app is run, under access controls appropriate to that environment. The apps do not maintain a central server-side database of user content.

Sharing

The apps do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party. Network requests are made only to the providers whose APIs the app is calling (for example, Google), using your own credentials.

Revoking access

You can revoke an app's access to your Google account at any time from https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy is updated, the new version will be posted at this URL with a revised "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: pranav-apps@googlegroups.com.
